Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2, 2026  ·  Version 1.0

Your privacy matters deeply to us. Logos AI handles health-related conversations, and we treat that information with the utmost care and respect. This policy explains clearly what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. Who We Are

Logos AI is operated by Satya11 LLC, a company based in Nevada County, California. For privacy matters, you can reach us at alexblake.pires@gmail.com.

2. What Information We Collect

Category What We Collect Why

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver and improve the Logos AI consultation service
  • To save your conversation history and enable continuity across sessions
  • To authenticate your identity and manage your account
  • To personalize your experience based on your role (client, practitioner, etc.)
  • To communicate service updates or respond to your inquiries
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. AI Processing and Anthropic

Logos AI uses Anthropic's Claude API to power its AI responses. When you send a message, your input is processed by Anthropic's systems to generate a response. Anthropic's own privacy policy governs how they handle API data. We are actively pursuing a Zero Data Retention agreement with Anthropic to ensure your consultation content is not stored on their servers.

We do not share your personal identity with Anthropic — only the text content of your messages as needed to generate responses.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL), hosted on infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit. Conversation data is associated with your user account and protected by row-level security controls.

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of health-related information. However, no system is perfectly secure — please use a strong, unique password for your account.

6. Health Information

Logos AI may collect health-related information that you voluntarily share in consultation sessions. This information is used solely to provide the service. We treat it with the highest level of care.

Logos AI is not a covered entity under HIPAA in the traditional medical sense; however, we are committed to health data privacy principles and handle your information accordingly. We do not share consultation content with third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — Database and authentication (supabase.com)
  • Anthropic — AI language model API (anthropic.com)
  • Railway — Application hosting (railway.app)
  • Stripe — Payment processing (stripe.com) — where applicable

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing their data handling. We encourage you to review them.
